# OpenOrion2 player's manual: what is new

This manual is for players. It describes how OpenOrion2 differs from
Master of Orion II 1.40b23, the version GOG and Steam sell, the way the
manual of the unofficial 1.50/1.51 patch describes that patch: what
changed, why, how to use it, with examples.

Much of what is new here comes from the community patch 1.51. Its
features come over one at a time, and not always exactly as 1.51 does
them. Every chapter ends with a "Compared with 1.51" note saying where we
differ, so a player who knows 1.51 knows what to expect.

The specs under `docs/screens/`, `docs/turn/` and the rest are for the
port's developers; this manual is not. When a feature lands, its chapter
here lands with it.

## Chapters

The chapters follow the 1.51 manual's order. A chapter appears when it
has something in it.

1. [Build Queue Management](build-queue.md): build lists on the keys Q
   and 0-9, taking a purchase back.
2. [Ships and Fleets](ships.md): the sixth ship design.
3. [Critical Bug Fixes](bug-fixes.md): crashes, freezes and network
   desyncs.
4. [Keyboard Shortcuts](keyboard.md): the keys that differ from
   1.40b23.

Still to come, as features land: Compatibility (saves, network play),
Critical Bug Fixes, Interface Changes, Gameplay Changes (galaxy
generation, tactical and strategic combat, ships, production, research,
spying, AI and diplomacy, population, leaders, events).

## How a chapter is written

- Start from what the player sees and does, not from the code: the
  screen, the key, the result.
- Say what 1.40b23 did when it matters to understand the change.
- Give examples a player can try.
- End with "Compared with 1.51": what 1.51 does differently, what we left
  out or changed, and why. "Same as 1.51" is a fine note.
- Tag what came from where: *(from 1.51)* for a patch feature, *(new in
  OpenOrion2)* for one of our own.
