# The port's own art

Nothing here comes from the original game or is derived from its files:
the game's own graphics, texts, sounds and videos stay in your copy of
Master of Orion II, in its LBX archives, and are read from there at run
time. These are the few pictures and fonts the port adds for what the
original never had, the online lobby, embedded in the binary by package
`assets`.

- `lobby/button.png`: the lobby's metal buttons. Made from scratch for
  this project, in the style of the original's interface; cut into caps
  and a middle tile by `tools/buttoncut`.
- `lobby/font/`: the lobby's Unicode text, the Fusion Pixel Font, a free
  font under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (its `README.md`, `OFL.txt` and
  `LICENSES/`).
